Web of Science retrieval queries — mangrove remote sensing corpus
===============================================================

Database : Web of Science Core Collection
Search   : TS (Topic)
Coverage : January 1972 – 15 July 2026
Filters  : English; document types = article, proceedings paper, review
Result   : 2,332 publications after DOI deduplication

NOTES
-----
* The 2026 slice ends on 15 July 2026 and is therefore partially indexed.
  It is excluded from trend fitting throughout the manuscript.
* The corpus itself is NOT redistributable (Clarivate licence). Only the
  derived tables in this archive are released; re-run these queries against
  your own institutional subscription to obtain the source records.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIMARY QUERY — mangrove remote sensing corpus (2,332 publications)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

TS=(("mangrove*" OR "mangal" OR "rhizophoraceae") AND ("remote sensing" OR "earth observation" OR "satellite imagery" OR "aerial photography" OR "airborne" OR "UAV" OR "UAS" OR "drone" OR "LiDAR" OR "SAR" OR "synthetic aperture radar" OR "optical remote sensing" OR "multispectral" OR "hyperspectral" OR "radar" OR "InSAR" OR "PolSAR" OR "RGB"))


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PARENT-FIELD QUERY — remote sensing, mangrove block removed
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

The same string with the mangrove term block deleted, so that mangrove papers
sit *inside* the denominator. Used for the parent-field normalization
(RRMI_norm, A_c) of Tables S7–S8; ΣP = 1,035,922 record counts over
200 countries/regions.

TS=(("remote sensing" OR "earth observation" OR "satellite imagery" OR "aerial photography" OR "airborne" OR "UAV" OR "UAS" OR "drone" OR "LiDAR" OR "SAR" OR "synthetic aperture radar" OR "optical remote sensing" OR "multispectral" OR "hyperspectral" OR "radar" OR "InSAR" OR "PolSAR" OR "RGB"))

Export the parent-field result as **Refine results → Countries/Regions** (or
Analyze results → Countries/Regions); both act on the whole result set.
The archived copy of that export is
external/parent_field_country_counts.xlsx.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXTERNAL DATA — mangrove area
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

High-resolution Global Mangrove Forest (HGMF) 2020 map, 10 m
(Jia et al. 2023) — publicly available; cite the source, do not
redistribute the raster. Per-country aggregates derived here:
external/HGMF_country_area.csv
