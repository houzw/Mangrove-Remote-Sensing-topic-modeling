## P0-2 Study-area vs affiliation



Re-parsing the raw WoS records to recover the first author's country (the corpus keeps only the union of co-author countries), 148 of 1353 (10.9%) high-output-country first-authored papers name at least one low-output tropical study region in their title or abstract. Restricting to the 799 papers with any detectable study region, the fraction rises to 18.5%. This confirms the affiliation-vs-study-area confounding: a substantial share of the over-publishing countries' output reports fieldwork in resource-rich tropics, which is why we phrase the result as a first-author output deficit rather than an under-studied claim. China is the leading case: 3.8% of 503 Chinese-first-authored papers name a low-output tropical study region.
